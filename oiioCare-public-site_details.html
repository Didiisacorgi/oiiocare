About

oiioCare is a small tool for recording the relationship between appetite thoughts and emotions.

It is not an app for restricting your diet. It is a little parrot that helps you understand “why I want to eat.”

When you suddenly really want to eat, just tap the “emergency button.” Little Parrot will help you record your appetite intensity, your mood such as boredom, anxiety, anger, or loneliness, whether you ate or did not eat, and how the timeline changes through the day.

Over time, you may notice that your appetite has patterns. Sometimes it is not hunger. Sometimes it is a need for a little gentle attention.

We believe every appetite is not an enemy. It may be an emotion, a piece of pressure, or a quiet longing. Understanding it is where self-healing begins.

Little Parrot Appetite hopes to become your awareness partner. When you feel anxious, binge, guilty, or simply want to sit quietly for a while, a little parrot stays by your side.

Does oiioCare replace treatment?

No. It is a daily companion and self-observation tool. If distress continues or gets worse, professional support is still important.

Why does it not emphasize calories?

Because the product focuses on emotions, situations, and sustainable care actions instead of creating more anxiety.
